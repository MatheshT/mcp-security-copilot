Here is a complete, pitch-ready framework for your hackathon. You can copy and paste this directly into your presentation slides. This is designed to sound futuristic, technically impressive, and highly commercial.

Project Name: NeuralSentinel AI
Tagline: The Autonomous Digital Immune System for SMEs.

1. The Problem
The Threat Evolution: Phishing is no longer just badly spelled emails. SMEs face AI-generated polymorphic attacks, malicious QR codes (Quishing), fake payment portals, and deepfake impersonation.
The SME Vulnerability Gap: Large enterprises spend millions on Security Operations Centers (SOCs). SMEs, schools, and consumers rely entirely on human vigilance—which is failing.
The Reactive Trap: Current security tools wait for a user to click a link, download a file, or paste data. By the time the alarm rings, the breach has already occurred.
2. The Solution
NeuralSentinel AI is a zero-trust, autonomous security layer that operates seamlessly in the user's browser.

Zero-Click Interception: Using DOM-level observation, the extension scans emails, URLs, QR codes, and payment requests before the user interacts with them.
Agentic AI: Powered by a LangChain autonomous agent, it doesn’t just scan text—it cross-references WHOIS data, domain age, and threat intelligence databases autonomously.
Multi-Modal Vision: Beyond text, it takes a visual "snapshot" of the webpage, using Vision AI to detect fake login portals and manipulated branding that traditional text-based scanners miss.
Physical UI Override: If a threat is detected, it physically alters the webpage, blurring malicious links and overlaying an interactive "Risk Shield" with a plain-English explanation of the attack.
3. Innovation (The "2050" Factor)
What makes this a winning hackathon project:

Autonomous AI Agents (Not just LLMs): We don’t just ask an LLM "is this safe?". We use LangGraph to give the AI tools. The AI autonomously decides: "I see a URL. I will check the domain age. It's 2 hours old. I will now analyze the email text for urgency. I will now compile a risk score."
Quishing (QR Phishing) Defense: Users can upload images or hold QR codes to their webcam. The AI decodes the destination, runs it through threat intel, and prevents the mobile "pointer" from clicking the malicious link.
Explainable AI (XAI): SMEs don't have security teams. Our AI doesn't just say "Blocked." It explains: "Risk Score: 95/100. This email creates false urgency and the payment link points to a domain registered in Russia 1 hour ago, mimicking your vendor 'Acme Corp'."
4. Vision Plan
Phase 1 (The Hackathon MVP): Browser extension with DOM interception for Gmail/Webmail, LLM text analysis, and visual UI override (blurring malicious elements).
Phase 2 (6-12 Months): Multi-Channel Integration. The AI layer expands beyond the browser to monitor SMS (via SMS forwarding API), Slack/Teams, and WhatsApp, centralizing all threat data into an SME dashboard.
Phase 3 (2030+): Predictive Swarm Intelligence. Moving from reactive to predictive. By analyzing domain registration patterns globally, NeuralSentinel will predict and pre-block zero-day phishing domains hours before the attack campaign even begins.
5. UI/UX Design
Design Aesthetic: "Cyberpunk meets Corporate Enterprise." Dark mode, neon accents, holographic overlays. (Think of the UI in the movie Iron Man or Minority Report).

User Experience Flow (The Employee Side):

Safe State: The extension icon glows a calm, pulsing cyan.
Threat Detected: The screen subtly pulses. The malicious email or link is instantly wrapped in a blurred CSS container. Over it, a translucent, glowing red "Holographic Shield" appears.
The Alert: Inside the shield:
Risk Score: 94/100 - CRITICAL
Attack Type: Invoice Fraud / Typosquatting
AI Explanation: "The payment button points to 'paypa1.com' instead of 'paypal.com'. Do not interact."
Action Buttons: [Report to IT] / [Sandbox (Open safely)]
Admin Experience (The SME SOC Dashboard):

A separate web portal for the business owner/IT team.
Live Threat Map: A globe or network graph showing incoming blocked attacks in real-time.
Employee Risk Heatmap: Visualizes which employees are receiving the most blocked threats (identifying who needs training).
Zero-Trust Ledger: A chronological, immutable ledger of every threat blocked, who it targeted, and the AI’s reasoning.
